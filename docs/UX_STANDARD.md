# UX & Conversion Standard

Factory UX is business-psychology driven. Section order must follow visitor intent, trust formation and decision friction rather than a universal marketing-page formula.

## Required questions

Every archetype answers:

1. What brings the visitor here?
2. What proof is required before action?
3. What is the primary conversion?
4. What friction must be reduced?
5. Which information belongs before vs after proof?

## Conversion rules

- Use fewer, stronger CTAs instead of repetitive button spam.
- Never use fake urgency, hidden opt-outs or other dark patterns.
- Forms request only information needed at that decision stage.
- Complex B2B/architecture/construction leads use qualified briefs; local services use low-friction contact; restaurants/hotels use reservation flows.
- Proof must be factual and data-backed.
- Mobile journeys may shorten composition but must not remove decision-critical information.

## Archetype differentiation

Different archetypes must represent different user journeys, proof strategies or conversion architectures — not visual reskins.
