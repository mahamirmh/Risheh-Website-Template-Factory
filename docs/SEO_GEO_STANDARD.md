# SEO & GEO Standard

Every archetype must support both classic search discoverability and answer-engine readability without sacrificing UX.

## Baseline

- One clear search intent per primary page.
- Semantic heading structure and descriptive internal links.
- Canonical URLs, sitemap and robots policy.
- Structured data only when factual source data exists.
- Local businesses: consistent NAP, service area, opening hours, map/location data and local service pages where justified.
- Editorial content: author, publish/update dates, citations and topic hierarchy.
- Product/service pages: explicit offering, audience, outcomes, limitations and FAQs.
- Images: descriptive filenames, dimensions, lazy loading and meaningful alt text.

## GEO / answer engines

Content should expose concise factual summaries, entity relationships, service definitions, locations, process steps and FAQ answers in semantic HTML. Do not fabricate statistics, awards, reviews or credentials to increase perceived authority.

## Internationalization

RTL/LTR and multilingual versions require locale-aware metadata, canonical/hreflang strategy and translated slugs where appropriate. Locale variants must not silently duplicate incorrect geographic claims.
